# ConsentX

> ConsentX is an audit-defensible consent management platform (CMP). It provides geo-aware cookie consent, prior-script blocking, Google Consent Mode v2, Global Privacy Control, a region rule engine, DSAR and grievance handling, and tamper-evident consent receipts and evidence. Coverage across GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, India's DPDP Act, LGPD, PIPEDA, PDPA, POPIA and many more, in one platform.

Plain-English answer pages explain the privacy and consent law in every country, the authority that enforces it, and how to comply. Pages are available in 37 languages with hreflang.

Full reference with summaries: https://www.consentx.io/llms-full.txt

## Product features
- [Cookie Consent Banner & CMP](https://www.consentx.io/features/consent-banner): Create a compliant cookie consent banner with granular privacy choices and category-based consent controls.
- [Prior-Script Blocking for Cookie Consent](https://www.consentx.io/features/prior-script-blocking): Block non-essential cookies, trackers, and scripts before consent to support privacy and cookie compliance.
- [Google Consent Mode v2 for Consent Management](https://www.consentx.io/features/google-consent-mode-v2): Keep measurement working while you enforce every choice.
- [Global Privacy Control (GPC) Support](https://www.consentx.io/features/global-privacy-control): Automatically honor Global Privacy Control (GPC) signals and respect browser-based privacy opt-out preferences.
- [Region Rule Engine for Geo-Aware Consent Management](https://www.consentx.io/features/region-rule-engine): Apply geo-aware consent rules by region and adapt privacy requirements without changing or shipping code.
- [DSAR & Grievance Management](https://www.consentx.io/features/dsar): Manage data subject access requests and grievances with workflows that help meet privacy compliance deadlines.
- [Consent Records, Receipts & Audit Evidence](https://www.consentx.io/features/consent-receipts): Prove consent for any visitor and date.
- [xScan-AI privacy scanner](https://www.consentx.io/features/xscan-ai): See what is firing on your site before consent.
